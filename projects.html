01 / Electrical work

Electrical Disconnect Switch Relocation – NEC Compliance

This project involved relocating an outdoor air conditioning disconnect switch that was installed high on the building. We moved the disconnect closer to ground level to improve accessibility and safety.

We also rerouted the electrical wiring through protective conduit to reduce exposed wiring, provide protection outdoors, and create a cleaner, more professional installation.

The project’s compliance goals focused on switch operating-handle height and disconnect accessibility, with NEC 404.8(A) and NEC 440.14 identified in the project brief.
