A look at my electrical project experience as an Assistant Electrician.

01 / Electrical work

Electrical Disconnect Switch Relocation – NEC Compliance

This project involved relocating an outdoor air conditioning disconnect switch that was installed high on the building. We moved the disconnect closer to ground level to improve accessibility and safety.

We also rerouted the electrical wiring through protective conduit to reduce exposed wiring, provide protection outdoors, and create a cleaner, more professional installation.

The project’s compliance goals focused on switch operating-handle height and disconnect accessibility, with NEC 404.8(A) and NEC 440.14 identified in the project brief.

02 / Troubleshooting and circuit installation

Electrical Panel Upgrade & Dedicated AC Circuit Installation

We identified an overheating circuit breaker associated with excessive electrical loads on a single circuit. To address the issue, we installed additional circuit breakers and redistributed the loads to reduce overloading and improve electrical safety.

We also ran new wiring and installed a dedicated circuit for the apartment’s new air conditioning system. This gave the AC its own circuit rather than adding its load to an existing shared circuit.

The project involved electrical troubleshooting, circuit installation, and wire management, with the goal of improving the reliability of the apartment’s electrical system.

03 / Wiring and switch installation

Outdoor Lighting Switch Installation

This project involved installing a new light switch to control new exterior lighting for the building. We extended wiring from an existing electric baseboard heater circuit to supply the new switch.

The wiring was routed through the walls to keep it concealed and maintain a clean, professional appearance. The project involved electrical wiring, circuit extensions, switch installation, and hands-on problem-solving.
