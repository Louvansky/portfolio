Skills

Practical skills.
Field experience.

My work as an Assistant Electrician involves troubleshooting, wiring and installation, and solving problems in the field. The projects in this portfolio show how these skills come together, from relocating a disconnect to helping install a dedicated AC circuit and a new lighting switch.

01 / Troubleshooting

Working through problems.

Troubleshooting is one of the practical skills I use as an Assistant Electrician. In the apartment panel project, our work began with identifying an overheating circuit breaker and the loads connected to that circuit. This project brought together investigation and practical installation work to address the issue.

02 / Wiring and installation

Hands-on electrical work.

My wiring and installation experience includes running wiring for a dedicated AC circuit, routing wiring through walls for a lighting switch, and helping relocate an outdoor disconnect. These projects also involved wire management, including concealed routes and protective conduit for a cleaner installation.

03 / Field problem-solving

Solving problems on the job.

Field problem-solving means working through the specific needs of an installation. My projects have involved improving access to a disconnect, redistributing electrical loads, and routing wiring to a new switch location. Each project called for attention to the existing setup and the purpose of the changes we were making.

04 / Areas of interest

Exploring technology and analysis.

Beyond my practical electrical skills, I’m interested in programming, CAD, electrical engineering, and sports analytics. These areas connect with my enjoyment of both hands-on work and analytical thinking. They are interests I’m exploring alongside my SDET major and Business minor at NJIT.
