About Me

Hands-on work.
Curious thinking.

I’m Louvansky Benjamin, an SDET major at New Jersey Institute of Technology with a minor in Business. I also work as an Assistant Electrician at my father’s company, American Electrical Technologies INC.

01 / Hands-on interests

Wires, design, and electrical systems.

I enjoy hands-on work with wires and the practical problem-solving that comes with electrical work. My role as an Assistant Electrician gives me opportunities to take part in projects involving wiring, circuit installation, and troubleshooting. I also have an interest in electrical engineering and computer-aided design (CAD), which connect my enjoyment of hands-on work with the design side of technical projects.

Looking ahead, I plan to pursue a master’s degree in Electrical Engineering after completing my undergraduate studies. This goal connects my interest in electrical systems with the practical experience I’m gaining as an Assistant Electrician.

02 / Analytical interests

Sports, analytics, and programming.

Sports analytics is one of my main interests because it brings together sports and analytical thinking. Alongside that interest, I enjoy programming and exploring the connection between technology and problem-solving. These interests sit alongside my electrical work and studies, giving me both practical and analytical areas to explore.
